## Outreach cadence (run JARVIS-led)

| Week | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Find named BD contact per company via LinkedIn (avoid generic inboxes) |
| 1 | Send initial 6 emails (stagger M-W-F, not all at once) |
| 2 | First follow-up on non-replies (CHUMMO drafts: 80 chars, single question, no full re-pitch) |
| 3 | Second follow-up — LinkedIn voice note + a different angle ("would a case study from TMMT help your team evaluate?") |
| 4 | Mark cold; rotate to next 6 partners |

**Don't multi-thread the same company.** One contact at a time per partner. Multi-threading reads like an agency blast.

## Discovery call agenda (when one books)

JARVIS prep brief, 5 sections, 25 min:

1. (5 min) AIXMOS context — who we serve, member economics
2. (5 min) Their fit — why we picked them specifically
3. (10 min) Mechanics — referral structure, integration, branding
4. (3 min) Next-step ask — agreement template + 30-day pilot
5. (2 min) Close — calendar a 1-month check-in

Get a yes/no on a 30-day pilot before you leave the call.
