AIXMOS AI BRAIN — SIMPLE GUIDE
==============================

FOR EVERYONE (including kids):

  1. Double-click:  AI Brain   (on Desktop)
     OR open:       D:\AIXMOS-AGENTS\AI-BRAIN.bat

  2. Press 1 to Turn Brain ON

  3. Press 2 to Ask the Brain a question

  4. Press 3 to see Who is Waiting

  5. Press 4 when you are done for the day


ONE-TIME (grown-up only):

  1. Install Docker Desktop — leave it running on the home PC

  2. Run MAKE-DESKTOP-BRAIN.bat  (puts "AI Brain" on Desktop)

  3. Copy config\aixmos.env.example  to  .env
     Fill in:
       ANTHROPIC_API_KEY
       SUPABASE_URL + SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY
       TMMT_OPS_URL + AGENT_WEBHOOK_SECRET

  4. Done. Kids only use AI-BRAIN.bat after that.


WHAT THE BRAIN IS:

  Your home Windows PC runs the helpers (n8n, etc.).
  CHUMMO talks to customers. MOOSE gets things done.
  JARVIS answers questions so you do not repeat yourself.
