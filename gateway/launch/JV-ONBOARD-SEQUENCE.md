# JV "Onboard-a-Car" Sequence (SOP) — grow your managed fleet

JV leads = car owners who let TMMT manage/rent their vehicle for a revenue split. Each one you
close becomes **rental inventory you earn on for months.** Maps to the GHL "Joint Venture Leads"
pipeline: New Lead → Lead Reply → Book Appointment → Vehicle Inspection → Pass/Fail → Install Tracker → Onboard to Fleet.

## Stage 1 — New Lead → first touch (51 sitting here = cold cash)
**Text/DM:**
> Hi {name}, this is [You] with **TMMT Rentals**. You expressed interest in earning passive income from your car — we manage everything (renters, payments, maintenance) and you collect a monthly check. Got 2 minutes this week to see if your car qualifies? Reply **YES**.
→ On reply, move card to **Lead Reply**.

## Stage 2 — Lead Reply → qualify + book (13 here)
- Confirm: year/make/model (we want **2013 & newer**), mileage, title status, condition, location.
- Explain the split + what TMMT covers. Answer objections.
- Book the **vehicle inspection** appointment → move to **Book Appointment**.

## Stage 3 — Book Appointment → inspection scheduled (7 here = HOTTEST)
- Lock date/time/location. Confirm what to bring (title, registration, keys, insurance).
- Reminder text 24h + 1h before. → move to **Vehicle Inspection** when scheduled/arrived.

## Stage 4 — Vehicle Inspection
- Inspect mechanically + cosmetically; photos; verify VIN/title/registration.
- **Pass** → proceed. **Fail** → log reason, offer fixes or decline (move to Fail Inspection).

## Stage 5 — Pass Inspection → agreement
- Sign the **JV / revenue-split management agreement** (term, split %, who pays maintenance/insurance, payout schedule, exit terms).
- ⚠️ Get this agreement reviewed by counsel once (it's a recurring financial contract).

## Stage 6 — Install Tracker
- Install GPS tracker; record device + vehicle in your system.

## Stage 7 — Onboard to Fleet
- Add the car to the **Fleet** table (Airtable): year/make/model, partner name, partner %, status, pricing.
- Now it's bookable → match it to your **rental waitlist** (the inventory-waiters).
- The owner shows in Airtable as a managed/closed JV partner.

## The flywheel
More JV cars onboarded → more rental inventory → fill the customer waitlist → revenue on **both** sides
(rental income + your management split). **Working the 7 "Book Appointment" JV leads is the highest-ROI
activity in the business** — each becomes months of compounding revenue.
