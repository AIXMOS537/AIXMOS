## Your playbook (read this first, then keep it open)
Your day runs on the **[vertical] playbook** -> see `../playbooks/<vertical>.md`. Follow every step. The checklists are how we keep quality high and how you level up.

## Your first week (training path)
1. Read your playbook end to end
2. Shadow (watch a senior operator do it)
3. Do tasks supervised — manager checks each one
4. Get cleared to work solo once you pass the quality bar

## How you become Top-Tier (your scorecard)
You're scored weekly on 4 things — see `../scorecards/`:
- 📋 **Following the playbook** (don't skip steps)
- ⚡ **Speed** (respond fast)
- 📦 **Output** (get the work done)
- ✅ **Quality** (few redos/complaints)
Hit all four and you climb: 🥉 Rookie -> 🥈 Operator -> 🥇 Senior -> 💎 Top-Tier. Higher tiers = more trust, more access, more responsibility.
