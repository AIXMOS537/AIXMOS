# 💰 Sales / Leasing — Playbook
_Part of the [[operator-os]] vertical playbooks. Pairs with [[tmmt-system]], [[people-hub]], [[onboarding-offboarding]]._
_Steps below are **DRAFT defaults** — correct anything that doesn't match how TMMT actually runs._

## What this vertical does
Lead generation, outreach, qualifying, closing, and follow-up across the rental/leasing pipeline.

## Systems it touches
TMMT app (`incoming_leads` -> `active_customers`) · GHL (`ghl_contacts`, pipelines: Rentals / Restoration-LTO) · OpenPhone (calls/SMS) · Gmail (email outreach) · Slack (handoffs).

## Roles
sales.

## SOP — Lead to close (pipeline stages)
1. **New** — lead lands -> log in GHL pipeline + `incoming_leads`. First touch FAST (see KPIs).
2. **Contacted** — reached the person. Capture: what they need, when, budget signal.
3. **Qualified** — fits a real offer (right vehicle/terms/timeline, passes basic screen).
4. **Quoted / Offer** — present the offer clearly (vehicle, rate, terms, deposit).
5. **Negotiation** — handle objections; restate value, not just price.
6. **Won** -> convert to `active_customers`, hand off to rentals/dispatch for fulfillment.
   **Lost** -> log the reason (price, timing, ghosted, etc.) for pattern-spotting.

## Follow-up cadence (don't let leads go cold)
- [ ] Day 0: first touch (call + text)
- [ ] Day 1, Day 3, Day 7: follow-ups, mixing call / text / email
- [ ] Then weekly until a clear yes/no
- [ ] Every contact logged + next-step dated in GHL

## Daily
- [ ] Work the pipeline by stage
- [ ] Contact everyone due today; no lead left without a next step
- [ ] Update stages so the board reflects reality

## The 4 KPIs (-> [[operator-scorecard-template]])
- 📋 **SOP adherence:** stages updated, follow-up cadence honored, reasons logged
- ⚡ **Speed:** lead -> first-touch time
- 📦 **Output:** calls/touches made, deals closed
- ✅ **Quality:** close rate, lead->customer conversion %, clean CRM data

## Training path
Read playbook + scripts -> shadow live calls -> role-play objections -> run pipeline supervised -> hit close-rate target = cleared solo.
