# 🧳 Make a Go-Kit flashdrive (from the Mac)

The Go-Kit is the two files in **this `gokit` folder**: `GO.html` + `START-HERE.txt`.

## On the Mac
1. Plug in the flashdrive — it mounts in Finder automatically.
2. Open this `gokit` folder (it's inside your `AIXMOS-Brain` once the brain is set up).
3. Drag **`GO.html`** and **`START-HERE.txt`** onto the flashdrive.
4. Done — eject and carry it. On any computer, double-click `GO.html`.

## ⚠️ ONE RULE
Copy **only this `gokit` folder's files** to a flashdrive — **NEVER the whole `AIXMOS-Brain` folder.** The brain holds private family/personal notes that must never go on a drive you plug into other people's machines. The Go-Kit is work-only and safe; the brain is not.

## Want it automated?
Open **Claude Code on the Mac** and say *"build a Go-Kit on my flashdrive"* — that session can copy it for you and confirm it's safe.
