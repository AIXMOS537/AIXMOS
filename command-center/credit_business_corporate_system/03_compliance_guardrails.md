# Compliance Guardrails

This is not legal advice. Use this as an operating checklist, then have an attorney review the final contracts, scripts, disclosures, and payment structure.

## Credit Repair Rules

Do not say:

- We guarantee deletions.
- We guarantee a score increase.
- We can remove accurate negative information.
- We can get you approved no matter what.
- Pay upfront for credit repair results.

Use safer language:

- We review your credit profile and help you understand options.
- We assist with dispute preparation when information may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unverifiable.
- Results vary based on the client's profile and creditor/reporting agency responses.
- We do not guarantee credit score changes, deletions, or approvals.

## Sales And Marketing Claims

Every marketing claim should be backed by documentation.

Avoid:

- Fake testimonials
- Unrealistic income claims
- Before/after score screenshots without context
- Guaranteed funding language
- Misleading urgency
- Saying approval is certain

Require:

- Written disclaimers
- Documented case studies
- Clear cancellation policy
- Written customer authorization
- Consent for calls/texts/emails

## Funding Rules

Do not:

- Fabricate business revenue.
- Alter bank statements.
- Encourage clients to lie on applications.
- Promise approval amounts.
- Represent yourself as a lender unless you are one.

Do:

- Prepare accurate documents.
- Explain lender criteria.
- Track submissions.
- Keep client approvals and denials documented.
- Separate consulting fees from lender fees.

## Car Rental Conversion Rules

Keep rental operations contractually separate from credit/funding services.

Required:

- Rental agreement
- Driver license verification
- Insurance requirements
- Payment terms
- Late fee policy
- Damage policy
- Vehicle return terms
- GPS/telematics disclosure if used

Avoid:

- Tying funding approval to rental participation.
- Promising rental income.
- Selling a business opportunity without proper disclosures.
- Blurring customer, partner, and employee roles.

## Corporate Controls

Implement these before scaling ads:

- Approved script library
- Approved offer sheet
- Contract version control
- Client file checklist
- Refund/dispute tracker
- Complaint log
- Weekly compliance review
- Team training checklist
