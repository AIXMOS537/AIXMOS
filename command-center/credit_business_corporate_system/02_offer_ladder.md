# Offer Ladder

## Level 1: Free / Low-Friction Entry

### Funding Readiness Scorecard

Purpose: collect lead data and identify whether the person needs credit repair, business structure, documents, income seasoning, or vehicle monetization.

Inputs:

- Personal credit score range
- Negative items
- Current business entity status
- Business bank account status
- Monthly revenue
- Tax documentation
- Current funding goal
- Vehicle/rental interest

Outcome:

- Score: Not Ready, Almost Ready, Funding Ready, Rental Candidate
- Next step: consultation booking

## Level 2: Credit Readiness Program

Purpose: help clients understand and improve their credit profile through compliant review, education, dispute preparation, and account strategy.

Important language:

- No guaranteed deletion.
- No guaranteed score increase.
- No promise to remove accurate information.
- Charge only according to applicable credit repair rules and after services are performed where required.

Deliverables:

- Credit profile audit
- Negative item review
- Dispute preparation support
- Credit education plan
- Monthly progress review
- Funding readiness checklist

## Level 3: Business Funding Readiness Package

Purpose: prepare entrepreneurs for business funding by organizing their business profile, documents, and lender-facing presentation.

Deliverables:

- Entity readiness review
- Business bank account checklist
- Address/phone/web presence checklist
- Revenue and bank statement review
- Document checklist
- Funding strategy map

Important language:

- No guaranteed approval.
- No guaranteed amount.
- No deceptive income or revenue claims.
- No fabricated documents.

## Level 4: Funding Packaging / Consulting

Purpose: support qualified clients with packaging and application readiness.

Deliverables:

- Funding profile summary
- Lender-fit checklist
- Application preparation
- Submission tracker
- Approval/denial review
- Next-step funding plan

## Level 5: Vehicle Monetization / Car Rental Conversion

Purpose: turn qualified clients into rental customers, vehicle partners, or operators.

Routes:

- Client rents vehicles for personal/business use.
- Client uses funding to acquire vehicle assets.
- Client becomes a fleet partner.
- Client enters lease-to-own or operator program if legally and financially suitable.

Required controls:

- Separate rental agreement.
- Insurance verification.
- Driver verification.
- Payment authorization.
- Clear vehicle responsibility terms.
- No guaranteed rental income claims.

## Recommended Corporate Package Names

- Fundability Audit
- Credit Readiness Program
- Business Funding Prep
- Funding Packaging Support
- Vehicle Monetization Pathway
- Fleet Partner Intake
