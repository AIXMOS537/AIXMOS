# Master Blueprint

## Corporate Positioning

The company should be positioned as a funding readiness and asset monetization company, not just a credit repair company.

Recommended positioning:

> We help entrepreneurs become fundable, structure their profile, and turn approved capital into income-producing vehicle opportunities.

Avoid positioning that promises guaranteed credit score increases, guaranteed approvals, guaranteed deletions, or guaranteed income.

## Business Model

The company has three revenue centers:

1. Credit readiness and repair support
2. Business funding preparation and consulting
3. Car rental customer acquisition, fleet usage, and operator conversion

The corporate goal is to make each revenue center independent enough to operate with its own team, SOPs, contracts, KPIs, and compliance rules.

## Customer Journey

1. Lead sees funding, credit, or vehicle-income content.
2. Lead completes a readiness form.
3. Lead books a consultation.
4. Sales team qualifies the lead.
5. Client enters credit/funding readiness program.
6. Operations team collects documents and performs credit/business review.
7. Client receives education, repair support, and funding preparation.
8. Qualified client is offered business funding support.
9. Funded or qualified client is routed into car rental use case:
   - rental customer
   - fleet partner
   - lease-to-own participant
   - operator candidate

## 90-Day Corporate Rollout

### Days 1-15: Foundation

- Finalize offer ladder.
- Separate credit repair, business funding, and rental documents.
- Build CRM pipeline stages.
- Create sales scripts and intake forms.
- Draft compliance-safe marketing language.

### Days 16-30: Operations

- Create SOPs for every repeated task.
- Build document collection checklist.
- Build client onboarding sequence.
- Build weekly KPI dashboard.
- Assign team roles and owners.

### Days 31-60: Acquisition

- Launch one core lead magnet.
- Launch booking funnel.
- Run outbound/referral campaigns.
- Add missed-call, no-show, and nurture automations.
- Track conversion from lead to booked call to paid client.

### Days 61-90: Backend Scale

- Segment clients by funding readiness.
- Add car rental conversion workflow.
- Launch referral program.
- Build partner outreach list.
- Prepare lender/partner capability deck.
- Review compliance and refund/dispute risk weekly.

## Immediate Priorities

1. Clean up the offer ladder.
2. Build the CRM pipeline.
3. Draft compliant scripts and agreements.
4. Build SOPs for onboarding, document collection, credit review, funding review, and rental conversion.
5. Track KPIs weekly.
