# Hiring And Training Infrastructure

## First Hires

### Operations Manager

Owns:

- Daily team execution
- SOP compliance
- KPI updates
- Client delivery oversight
- Escalations

### Appointment Setter

Owns:

- New lead speed-to-contact
- Booking calls
- No-show recovery
- CRM updates

### Client Intake Specialist

Owns:

- Onboarding
- Document collection
- Client folder setup
- Checklist completion

### Credit Specialist

Owns:

- Credit profile review
- Dispute preparation workflow
- Monthly client updates
- Credit education tasks

### Funding Specialist

Owns:

- Business readiness review
- Funding document checklist
- Application tracker
- Funding partner coordination

### Rental Coordinator

Owns:

- Rental application review
- Driver/insurance verification
- Vehicle scheduling
- Payment status

## Required Staff Sign-Offs

Every staff member should sign off on:

- No guarantee policy
- Client privacy policy
- CRM update expectations
- Call/script standards
- Document handling rules
- Refund/escalation process

## Role-Based Training Flow

New hire:

1. Watch company overview.
2. Complete compliance basics.
3. Complete CRM training.
4. Review role SOPs.
5. Pass role quiz.
6. Shadow calls/tasks.
7. Complete supervised work.
8. Receive manager sign-off.
