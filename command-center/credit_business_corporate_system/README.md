# Credit Repair + Business Funding Corporate System

This folder is the working operating system for scaling the company into a corporate, compliance-first financial services and vehicle monetization platform.

Core model:

1. Attract consumers and entrepreneurs who need credit/funding readiness.
2. Diagnose their credit, document, income, and business profile.
3. Deliver compliant credit education, repair support, and funding preparation.
4. Move qualified clients into business funding support.
5. Convert the right clients into car rental customers, partners, or operators.

Primary files:

- `01_master_blueprint.md` - corporate strategy, offer ladder, departments, and 90-day rollout.
- `02_offer_ladder.md` - service packages and customer journey.
- `03_compliance_guardrails.md` - practical compliance rules for credit repair, funding, sales, and marketing.
- `04_sales_funnel.md` - lead flow, pipeline stages, scripts, and automations.
- `05_sop_index.md` - SOP library outline for hiring and delegation.
- `06_kpi_scorecard.csv` - weekly scorecard fields to track growth.

Working principle:

Scale only what is documented, measurable, and compliant.
