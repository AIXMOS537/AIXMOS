# Sales Funnel

## Core Funnel

1. Ad, referral, content, or outbound message
2. Funding Readiness Scorecard
3. Booking page
4. Confirmation SMS/email
5. Consultation call
6. Paid onboarding
7. Document collection
8. Credit/funding review
9. Program delivery
10. Funding pathway
11. Car rental conversion

## CRM Pipeline Stages

1. New Lead
2. Contacted
3. Scorecard Completed
4. Appointment Booked
5. No-Show
6. Consultation Completed
7. Qualified
8. Not Qualified

10. Paid / Onboarded
11. Documents Needed
12. Credit Review Active
13. Funding Prep Active
14. Funding Submitted
15. Funding Approved
16. Funding Denied / Rework
17. Rental Candidate
18. Rental Customer
19. Partner Candidate
20. Closed / Inactive

## Consultation Call Flow

1. Confirm goal.
2. Identify current credit/funding problem.
3. Ask about business entity, revenue, bank statements, and documents.
4. Ask about vehicle or rental goals.
5. Explain realistic pathway.
6. State no guarantees.
7. Present the correct package.
8. Confirm next step.

## Core Qualification Questions

- What is your main goal: credit, funding, business setup, or vehicle income?
- What is your estimated credit score range?
- Do you have active negative items?
- Do you have an LLC or corporation?
- Do you have a business bank account?
- Do you have recent business bank statements?
- Do you have verifiable income or revenue?
- What funding amount are you seeking?
- What would you use funding for?
- Are you interested in renting vehicles, acquiring vehicles, or becoming a fleet partner?

## Automation Sequences

### New Lead

- Instant SMS
- Instant email
- Call attempt task
- Reminder if scorecard not completed

### Appointment Booked

- Confirmation SMS
- Calendar invite
- 24-hour reminder
- 2-hour reminder
- 15-minute reminder

### No-Show

- 5-minute follow-up SMS
- Reschedule link
- Next-day call task
- 3-day nurture

### Paid Client

- Welcome email
- Portal invite
- Document checklist
- Payment receipt
- First review appointment

### Rental Candidate

- Rental interest form
- Driver verification checklist
- Insurance/payment requirements
- Rental agreement workflow
