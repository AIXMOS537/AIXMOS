# Follow-Up Sequences

Keep messages short. Every message should ask for one clear action.

## New Lead

### Touch 1: Immediate

```text
Hey [Name], this is [Rep] with TMMT. I saw your request come through. What is the main thing you are trying to get handled right now?
```

### Touch 2: Two Hours Later

```text
Quick follow-up, [Name]. I can point you in the right direction, but I need to know what you are trying to solve first. Is this for [option A] or [option B]?
```

### Touch 3: Next Day

```text
Hey [Name], still need help with this or should I close the request for now?
```

## Proposal Sent

### 24 Hours

```text
Hey [Name], did you get a chance to review the details I sent? If you want to move forward, the next step is [action].
```

### 48 Hours

```text
Checking in before I update your file. Is the hold-up price, timing, or a question about how it works?
```

### 72 Hours

```text
I do not want to keep bothering you. Should I keep this open, or close it out for now?
```

## No-Show

```text
Hey [Name], looks like we missed each other. Do you want to reschedule for today or tomorrow?
```

## Payment Due

```text
Hey [Name], your next step is still pending. Once [payment/document/confirmation] is complete, we can move forward with [result]. Do you want me to resend the link?
```
