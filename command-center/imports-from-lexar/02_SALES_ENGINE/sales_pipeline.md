# Sales Pipeline

Every lead must have one status, one owner, and one next action.

## Pipeline Stages

New Lead:

- Lead has entered the system.
- Required next action: contact within 15 minutes during business hours.

Contacted:

- First contact has been made.
- Required next action: qualify or schedule call.

Qualified:

- Lead has need, budget, authority, and timeline.
- Required next action: book discovery or send next-step instructions.

Call Booked:

- Call is scheduled.
- Required next action: confirm attendance and prepare notes.

Proposal Sent:

- Offer or quote has been sent.
- Required next action: follow up within 24 hours.

Negotiation:

- Lead is asking about price, terms, timing, or details.
- Required next action: handle objection and ask for decision.

Closed Won:

- Payment, signed agreement, or confirmed commitment received.
- Required next action: onboarding.

Closed Lost:

- Lead declined, went silent, was unqualified, or chose another provider.
- Required next action: log reason and add nurture follow-up if appropriate.

Onboarding:

- Customer is handed to fulfillment.
- Required next action: confirm customer knows next steps.

## Required Fields

- Lead name
- Business line
- Source
- Phone
- Email
- Status
- Owner
- Estimated value
- Probability
- Next action
- Next action due
- Last touch date
- Objection
- Close lost reason
- Notes

## Daily Sales Rules

- New leads are touched within 15 minutes during business hours.
- Every open deal is touched or intentionally skipped every business day.
- No proposal sits more than 24 hours without follow-up.
- Every lost deal gets a reason.
- Every common objection goes into the objection bank.
