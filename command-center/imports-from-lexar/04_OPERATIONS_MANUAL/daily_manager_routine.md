# Daily Manager Routine

## Before 9:30 AM

- Open Airtable.
- Review Leads Deals.
- Review Team Tasks.
- Review Money Leaks.
- Review customer messages.
- Review yesterday's closeout.

## 9:30 AM Standup

Ask each person:

- What did you finish yesterday?
- What are you doing today?
- What is blocked?
- What could affect revenue or customers?

## 11:00 AM Sales Push

- Check new leads.
- Check open proposals.
- Check no-shows.
- Check unpaid next steps.
- Assign follow-ups.

## 1:00 PM Midday Rescue

- Find stuck deals.
- Find overdue tasks.
- Find unresolved customer issues.
- Find missing documents or payments.

## 4:30 PM Closeout

- Update statuses.
- Log decisions.
- Assign tomorrow's first tasks.
- Send escalations if needed.
- Save notes for weekly owner update.

## End-Of-Day Questions

- What money moved?
- What money is stuck?
- What customer issue is still open?
- What employee needs correction?
- What SOP needs updating?
