# TMMT Month Away Plan

This is the minimum operating system needed for TMMT to keep selling, training, fulfilling, and reporting while the owner is away for at least one month.

## The Goal

The business should run on a clear rhythm:

- Sales activity happens every business day.
- Every lead has a status, owner, and next action.
- Every employee has a daily checklist and training path.
- Every customer issue has an owner and escalation level.
- The owner gets a short weekly report instead of constant random messages.

## The First 7 Days Before Leaving

### Day 1: Name The Coverage Team

Assign these seats, even if one person holds more than one seat:

- Acting Manager: owns daily execution and team check-ins.
- Sales Lead: owns leads, calls, follow-ups, and closes.
- Operations Lead: owns fulfillment, customer issues, and SOP compliance.
- Money Lead: owns unpaid balances, bills, and money leaks.
- Admin Recorder: updates Airtable, decision log, and daily notes.

### Day 2: Load The Command Center

Create or import Airtable tables for:

- Leads Deals
- Team Tasks
- Employees
- Training Progress
- Decision Log
- Bills
- Income
- Expenses
- Money Leaks

Every active lead, unpaid balance, employee, and open task must be entered before departure.

### Day 3: Lock The Daily Rhythm

The Acting Manager runs:

- Morning standup.
- Midday rescue check.
- End-of-day closeout.
- Friday weekly owner update.

Use `00_COMMAND_CENTER/DAILY_MANAGER_DASHBOARD.md` and `prompts/TMMT_MONTH_AWAY_PROMPTS.md`.

### Day 4: Protect Sales

The Sales Lead must have:

- current offer
- lead sources
- pipeline stages
- discovery call script
- objection responses
- proposal template
- follow-up messages

Use `02_SALES_ENGINE/`.

### Day 5: Train The Team

Each active employee needs:

- role
- manager
- daily checklist
- success metric
- first 7-day training plan
- what they can decide without asking
- what they must escalate

Use `03_TRAINING_ACADEMY/`.

### Day 6: Write The Escalation Rules

Anything that could damage money, customers, legal standing, reputation, or access must be escalated.

Use `00_COMMAND_CENTER/ESCALATION_RULES.md`.

### Day 7: Run A Simulation

Have the team operate for one full day without you jumping in.

At the end of the day, ask:

- What did they ask you that should be in the system?
- Which decisions were unclear?
- Which sales steps were weak?
- Which tasks were not updated?
- What should be turned into an SOP before you leave?

## Owner Communication Rules

Default: the team should not message the owner constantly.

Use this ladder:

- Level 1: Team decides using SOPs.
- Level 2: Acting Manager decides and logs it.
- Level 3: Acting Manager sends daily summary.
- Level 4: Acting Manager escalates immediately.

Immediate escalation only for:

- chargebacks, refunds, disputes, legal threats, or fraud
- angry customers who may post publicly or cancel
- payment access, banking, card, domain, or system lockouts
- deals above the pre-approved discount or contract limit
- employee no-show that threatens customer delivery
- any decision over the approved spending limit

## Weekly Owner Update

Every Friday, send one report with:

- cash collected
- cash stuck
- deals opened, won, lost, and next actions
- customer issues
- team performance
- decisions made
- risks for next week
- owner decisions needed

Use `00_COMMAND_CENTER/WEEKLY_OWNER_UPDATE.md`.

## Success Standard

After 30 days, TMMT should be able to show:

- every lead touched
- every deal status updated
- every team member trained against a role
- every major decision logged
- every money leak assigned a prevention action
- every customer issue resolved or escalated
