# Partner Performance Scorecard

```text
Act as the operator measuring partner dealership performance.

Review partner dealership activity, bookings, payments, maintenance, and customer experience metrics.

Focus on:
- booking conversion rates and revenue per partner
- timeliness of customer follow-up and reservation confirmations
- maintenance completion and vehicle readiness
- payment collection and invoice compliance
- partner communication or escalation risks
- actions that must be taken to improve partner performance

Organize your output into:
- top 5 partner performance concerns
- partner scorecard summary with strengths and weaknesses
- recommended partner actions and support items
- risk factors for missed revenue or lost customer trust
- suggested KPI changes or operational guardrails
- the first 3 performance improvements to implement

Be direct, factual, and focused on business outcomes.
```
