# Daily Command Prompts

## Morning Command

```text
Act as my daily business operator.

Using the information I give you, tell me:
1. The top 5 actions that protect or create revenue today.
2. Who needs follow-up.
3. What money is due or overdue.
4. Which customer or team issue could turn into a bigger problem.
5. What I should personally handle.
6. What I should delegate.
7. What can wait.

Give me a simple schedule for today.
```

## Midday Rescue

```text
Act as my midday operator.

Find anything stuck:
- leads not contacted
- customers waiting
- unpaid balances
- cards or bills due soon
- team tasks not updated
- rentals not confirmed
- documents missing
- orders delayed

Tell me the fastest actions to take before the day ends.
```

## Customer Reply

```text
Draft a clear, professional reply to this customer.

Goal:
- protect the relationship
- move the deal forward
- get payment, documents, confirmation, or a decision
- keep the message short enough to send by text

Customer message:
[paste message]

Context:
[paste context]
```

## Team Delegation

```text
Turn this into team tasks.

For each task, give me:
- owner
- exact action
- due date
- priority
- what done looks like
- message I can send to assign it

Raw notes:
[paste notes]
```

## End Of Day Closeout

```text
Act as my end-of-day operator.

Summarize:
1. What got done.
2. What money moved.
3. What money is still stuck.
4. What needs follow-up tomorrow.
5. What I avoided or delayed.
6. What my team needs in the morning.
7. The first 3 things I should do tomorrow.
```
