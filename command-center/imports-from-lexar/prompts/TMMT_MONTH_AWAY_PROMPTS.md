# TMMT Month Away Prompts

## Away Morning Manager

```text
Act as the TMMT acting manager while the owner is away.

Using today's dashboard, identify:
1. Top 5 actions that protect or create revenue.
2. Leads or deals that need follow-up today.
3. Customer issues that could become expensive.
4. Team tasks that are overdue or unclear.
5. Employee training or correction needed today.
6. Decisions we can make without the owner.
7. Issues that must be escalated.

Give me a clean task list by owner with due times.
```

## Away Midday Rescue

```text
Act as the TMMT midday rescue operator.

Find anything stuck before the day gets away from us:
- new leads not contacted
- proposals not followed up
- customers waiting
- unpaid balances
- overdue tasks
- missing documents
- employee blockers
- decisions not logged

Tell me the fastest actions to take before close of business.
```

## Away Weekly Owner Report

```text
Create the weekly owner update for TMMT.

Include:
1. Overall status: Green, Yellow, or Red.
2. Cash collected and cash stuck.
3. Deals opened, won, lost, and still active.
4. Customer issues opened and resolved.
5. Team performance.
6. Training completed.
7. Decisions made without the owner.
8. Risks for next week.
9. Decisions needed from the owner.

Keep it concise and direct.
```

## SOP Builder

```text
Turn this messy process into a clear SOP.

Use this format:
- SOP name

- trigger

- step-by-step process
- what done looks like
- common mistakes
- escalation rule
- Airtable fields to update

Raw process:
[paste notes]
```

## Employee Correction

```text
Help me correct an employee clearly and professionally.

Context:
[paste what happened]

Give me:
1. The core issue.
2. The standard they missed.
3. A short message to send.
4. A retraining task.
5. How to track whether it improves.
```
