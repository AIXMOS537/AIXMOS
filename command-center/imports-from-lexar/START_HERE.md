# AIX AI Command System

This is your daily operating system for using AI to run your work, team, money, and three businesses with less chaos.

The goal is simple:

- Catch money leaks.
- Follow up faster.
- Know what matters today.
- Keep business and personal money visible.
- Give AI clear instructions so it can help you every day.

## The First Rule

Do not ask AI random questions all day.

Use AI like an operator:

1. Morning: decide what matters today.
2. Midday: catch stuck money and stuck tasks.
3. Evening: summarize the day and prepare tomorrow.
4. Weekly: review leaks, bills, cards, cash flow, and team execution.

## Your Core Tools

Use these as the base system:

- Airtable: source of truth for leads, rentals, clients, tasks, bills, cards, and expenses.
- ChatGPT or Claude: daily operator, writing, analysis, planning, and decisions.
- Codex: builds dashboards, APIs, scripts, and internal tools.
- Zapier or Make: connects forms, email, texts, payments, CRM, and Airtable.
- Vercel: hosts dashboards, portals, and internal tools.
- Phone shortcuts: capture voice notes, photos, receipts, and urgent tasks.

## What To Set Up First

Set up in this order:

1. Money command center.
2. TMMT month-away command center.
3. Daily AI operator prompts.
4. Follow-up and leak tracker.
5. Team task pipeline.
6. Business-specific automations.

If you need to be away for a month soon, open `TMMT_MONTH_AWAY_PLAN.md` before building anything else. That file is the coverage plan for keeping sales, training, operations, and owner updates moving while you are gone.

## Your Daily Rhythm

Morning:

```text
Run my daily command check-in. Tell me the top revenue priorities, urgent customer issues, unpaid money, overdue tasks, and what I should delegate today.
```

Midday:

```text
Review what has not moved today. Identify leads, payments, rentals, clients, bills, card balances, and team tasks that need action before close of business.
```

Evening:

```text
Summarize today. What money moved? What money is stuck? What did the team complete? What did I avoid? What must happen tomorrow morning?
```

Weekly:

```text
Review the last 7 days across my businesses and personal finances. Show leaks, wins, unpaid balances, bills due, expenses by category, follow-up failures, and next week's top 10 actions.
```

## Phone Setup

Put these on your phone home screen:

- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Airtable
- Bank apps
- Credit card apps
- Calendar
- Email
- Team chat
- Voice notes
- Receipt scanner or camera

Create three phone habits:

- Voice note every task instead of trying to remember it.
- Screenshot bills, balances, and customer issues.
- Send every money or customer issue into Airtable the same day.

## Flash Drive Setup

The flash drive is your portable business brain. It should store:

- This command system folder.
- SOPs.
- Prompts.
- Airtable templates.
- Team scripts.
- Business policies.
- Exported reports.
- Backup dashboard files.

Do not store raw API keys or passwords in plain text. Use a password manager.
