# Role Index

Train employees by role. Do not train people on random information first.

## Acting Manager

Mission:

- Keep the business moving while the owner is away.

Daily checklist:

- run morning standup
- review pipeline
- review overdue tasks
- review customer issues
- assign blockers
- log decisions
- send daily or weekly summaries

Success metrics:

- no lead without next action
- no overdue task without owner
- no customer issue without status
- weekly report sent on time

## Sales Lead

Mission:

- Convert leads into paid customers.

Daily checklist:

- contact new leads
- follow up open deals
- book calls
- send proposals
- handle objections
- update pipeline
- log lost reasons

Success metrics:

- speed to lead
- calls booked
- proposals sent
- close rate
- cash collected

## Operations Lead

Mission:

- Make sure customers get what was promised.

Daily checklist:

- review new closed deals
- confirm customer next steps
- track fulfillment
- resolve customer issues
- update SOP gaps
- escalate risks

Success metrics:

- on-time fulfillment
- customer issue resolution time
- fewer repeated mistakes
- SOP updates completed

## Admin Recorder

Mission:

- Keep the system accurate.

Daily checklist:

- update Airtable
- clean missing fields
- log decisions
- attach notes
- confirm task owners and due dates
- prepare reports

Success metrics:

- records updated same day
- no active lead missing next action
- no task missing owner
- no decision left undocumented

## Money Lead

Mission:

- Protect cash and stop leaks.

Daily checklist:

- check unpaid money
- check bills due
- review card risks
- follow up balances
- log leaks
- assign prevention actions

Success metrics:

- cash collected
- overdue money reduced
- bills paid on time
- leaks fixed
