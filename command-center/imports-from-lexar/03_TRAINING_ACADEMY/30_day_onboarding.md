# 30 Day Onboarding

Use this for every employee.

## Day 1

- Explain TMMT mission and current priorities.
- Assign role and manager.
- Add employee to Employees table.
- Give tool access needed for the role.
- Review escalation rules.
- Assign first daily checklist.

## Days 2 To 7

Employee must learn:

- daily checklist
- status updates
- customer communication standards
- task system rules
- sales or operations workflow for their role
- what to escalate
- how to update Airtable

Manager must verify:

- employee can complete checklist without reminders
- employee knows how to ask for help
- employee updates records correctly
- employee can explain what done looks like

## Week 2

Employee owns real work with review.

Focus:

- speed
- accuracy
- communication
- judgment
- follow-through

## Week 3

Employee owns real work with less review.

Manager checks:

- missed tasks
- late updates
- customer tone
- decision quality
- repeated mistakes

## Week 4

Employee should be able to train the next person on the basics of their role.

Final review:

- role score
- strengths
- risks
- next training path
- whether they can be trusted while owner is away
