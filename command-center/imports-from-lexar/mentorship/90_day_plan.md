90-Day Mentorship Plan

Overview

This plan is built for a 90-day mentorship cycle that combines: transformational personal growth, faith-centered discipline, and TMMT business training for car rental operations and entrepreneurship.

Structure

- 12 weekly modules plus 6 wrap-up days
- Each week has a primary theme, measurable daily actions, and check-ins
- Use the weekly lesson files under `lessons/` for the detailed activities

Weekly rhythm

Week 1: Foundation & intake
Week 2: Discipline, routine, and emotional control
Week 3: Spiritual grounding and mental toughness
Week 4: Communication, accountability, and support
Week 5: Car rental offer & high-ticket mentorship sales skills
Week 6: CRM, follow-up, and getting clients into the pipeline
Week 7: Fleet, money watch, and operational readiness
Week 8: SOPs, systems, handoffs, and delegation
Week 9: Credit, funding, and business education
Week 10: Growth, scaling, and retention
Week 11: Leadership, coaching others, and team building
Week 12: Legacy, calibration, and next 90-day launch
Wrap-up: review, commit, and plan the next cycle

Daily format

Each day use these three steps:
1. Morning: review the daily focus from the weekly lesson
2. Action: complete the micro-task and mark it done
3. Evening: write a short summary in the daily journal section

Daily habit checklist

- Morning prayer/reflection or grounding exercise
- 20 minutes of movement or disciplined work
- One accountability update to mentor/operator
- One clear milemarker completed
- Short journal note on progress and obstacles

90-day success metrics

- 12 weekly wins completed
- 90 daily accountability entries
- Launch of one car rental or business pilot project
- Clear documentation of team handoff and operations workflow
- Owner/operator daily report format created and used
