Mentorship Curriculum — From Trauma to Transformation + TMMT Business Track

Program length: 6 months (modular)
Audience: Troubled youth mentorship (Dashan blueprint), plus a parallel track for entrepreneurs (TMMT rentals & business education)

Core pillars

1. Personal Transformation
  - Emotional regulation
  - Faith and spiritual grounding
  - Discipline, routine, and physical health
  - Journaling, reflection, and accountability

2. Leadership & Life Skills
  - Communication, conflict resolution, relationships
  - Time management, goal-setting, and habit formation

3. Business & Income Track (TMMT)
  - Vehicle rental operations basics
  - Customer & CRM management
  - Money management and simple accounting
  - Lead generation and sales script (car rental mentorship script)

4. Systems & Delegation
  - SOPs, playbooks, and operator handoffs (use TMMT Operator Team Playbook)
  - Automations and low-friction tools for daily updates

Weekly structure (example)

- Week start: goal setting, scripture/reading, workout plan
- Midweek: skill session (business, credit, or habits)
- End of week: accountability check, metrics, and owner/operator report

Deliverables

- Weekly lesson notes (editable in `lessons/`)
- Daily prompts for the operator/mentor to run (see `prompts/`) 
- Live-call sales script for enrollment (based on `CAR RENTAL MENTORSHIP SCRIPT`)
