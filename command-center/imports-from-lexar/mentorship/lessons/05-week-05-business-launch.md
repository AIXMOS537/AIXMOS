Week 05 — Business Launch: Sales, Offer, and Enrollment

Objectives
- Learn the high-ticket mentorship call structure.
- Start selling the car rental mentorship offer.
- Create a simple onboarding sequence for new clients.

Daily focus
- Day 29: Review the sales script and role-play the first 5 questions.
- Day 30: Identify 3 potential clients or leads to contact.
- Day 31: Practice the offer delivery and investment close.
- Day 32: Build a one-page offer sheet with price, outcomes, and next steps.
- Day 33: Send the first outreach message to a prospect.
- Day 34: Track responses and prepare follow-up language.
- Day 35: Review any enrollment objections and write answers.

Key tools
- `CAR RENTAL MENTORSHIP SCRIPT`
- Enrollment call checklist
- Objection response templates
