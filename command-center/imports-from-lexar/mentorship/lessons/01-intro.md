Lesson 01 — Program Introduction

Objectives:
- Explain program mission: From Trauma to Transformation
- Establish expectations, schedule, and measurement
- Collect baseline intake: emotional state, daily routine, immediate risks

Activities:
- Intro call (30 min)
- Parent/guardian check-in
- Intake form: goals, current problems, immediate safety flags

Daily micro-tasks:
- Journaling: 3 pages for 7 days
- Short breathing and grounding routine (5min) twice daily
- Physical activity: 20 minutes daily
