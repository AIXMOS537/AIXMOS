Week 08 — Systems, SOPs, and Delegation

Objectives
- Document repeatable processes.
- Build checklists and simple SOPs.
- Hand off work with clear ownership and accountability.

Daily focus
- Day 50: Pick one repeatable task and write a one-page SOP.
- Day 51: Map who owns what in the team playbook.
- Day 52: Create a handoff checklist for the next shift.
- Day 53: Review one automation or workflow and note how to improve it.
- Day 54: Train one helper on a single business rule.
- Day 55: Publish an approval process for customer changes.
- Day 56: Conduct a mini SOP review and update one item.

Key tools
- Operator handoff checklist
- Simple SOP template
- Approval and escalation flow
