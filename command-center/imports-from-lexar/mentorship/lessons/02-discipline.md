Lesson 02 — Discipline & Emotional Control

Objectives:
- Build a daily routine
- Practice emotional regulation techniques
- Introduce accountability checkpoints

Activities:
- Daily check-ins (5 min morning, 5 min evening)
- Weekly 1-on-1 reflection call
- Short guided meditation and journaling prompt

Templates:
- Accountability check message to parent/operator
- Daily habit tracker (simple table)
