Lesson 03 — Business Track: Car Rental Basics

Objectives:
- Teach the core revenue model for TMMT Rentals
- Train on the car rental sales script and intake flow
- Set up basic CRM and follow-up process

Materials:
- `CAR RENTAL MENTORSHIP SCRIPT` (use as live-call template)
- Simple intake form fields: name, phone, interest, availability, current income

Activities:
- Role-play the $3K sales call using the script
- Create a 30/60/90 day action plan for launching a rental unit
- Assign one operator to the daily follow-up lane
