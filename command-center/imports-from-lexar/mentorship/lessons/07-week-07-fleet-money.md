Week 07 — Fleet, Money Watch, and Risk Control

Objectives
- Monitor vehicle readiness, expense risk, and cash flow.
- Create a daily money watch checklist.
- Protect the operation from customer, vehicle, and payment risk.

Daily focus
- Day 43: Review every vehicle status and update the fleet board.
- Day 44: Check payments due, overdue balances, and refunds.
- Day 45: Log any safety or inspection flags immediately.
- Day 46: Review the daily cash snapshot and escalation list.
- Day 47: Confirm gate/vehicle handover procedures with the team.
- Day 48: Create one procedure for an overdue customer payment.
- Day 49: Run a “what could break today?” risk check.

Key tools
- Fleet status dashboard
- Money watch checklist
- Risk escalation template
