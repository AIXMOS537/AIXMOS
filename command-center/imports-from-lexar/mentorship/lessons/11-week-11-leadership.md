Week 11 — Leadership, Coaching, and Team Building

Objectives
- Strengthen leadership habits and coaching skills.
- Teach a student how to lead others.
- Build a stronger operator/team culture.

Daily focus
- Day 71: Review the leadership principles in the playbook.
- Day 72: Coach someone else on a small task.
- Day 73: Run a quick team problem-solving session.
- Day 74: Share one lesson learned with the team.
- Day 75: Create a short leadership checklist.
- Day 76: Ask for feedback from a teammate or mentor.
- Day 77: Prepare a team accountability update.

Key tools
- Leadership checklist
- Coaching conversation template
- Team support scorecard
