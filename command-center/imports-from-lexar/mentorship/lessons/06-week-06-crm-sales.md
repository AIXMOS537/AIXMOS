Week 06 — CRM, Follow-Up, and Pipeline Management

Objectives
- Keep leads moving and follow-ups consistent.
- Track customer conversations with simple CRM rules.
- Use clear next actions for every contact.

Daily focus
- Day 36: Review all active leads and assign a next step to each.
- Day 37: Send a follow-up note to anyone who has not replied in 24 hours.
- Day 38: Add one new lead source and plan outreach for it.
- Day 39: Clean up the CRM notes and remove duplicates.
- Day 40: Practice the follow-up message with a team member.
- Day 41: Confirm appointment or call times for your top 3 prospects.
- Day 42: Create a simple daily CRM checklist for your team.

Key tools
- Lead status tracker
- Follow-up message templates
- CRM hygiene checklist
