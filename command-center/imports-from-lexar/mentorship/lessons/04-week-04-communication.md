Week 04 — Communication, Accountability, and Support

Objectives
- Strengthen honest communication and boundaries.
- Build a support network with mentors and family.
- Practice saying what you need and asking for help.

Daily focus
- Day 22: Share your three biggest challenges and goals with your mentor.
- Day 23: Practice active listening, ask one clarifying question in every conversation.
- Day 24: Write a gratitude note and send it to someone who helped you.
- Day 25: Review accountability commitments and update the daily habit tracker.
- Day 26: Record one moment when you chose patience over anger.
- Day 27: Create a short checklist for family or team check-in.
- Day 28: Do a live debrief call with your mentor and set next-week priorities.

Key tools
- Daily accountability message template
- Family/mentor support checklist
- Boundary statement practice script
