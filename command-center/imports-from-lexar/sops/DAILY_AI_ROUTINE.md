# Daily AI Routine

## Morning: 15 Minutes

1. Open Airtable.
2. Open bank and card apps.
3. Open ChatGPT or Claude.
4. Paste the Morning Command prompt.
5. Add dashboard updates, urgent messages, bills due, and team issues.
6. Let AI create your top priorities.
7. Put the top tasks into Airtable or your team task tool.

## Midday: 10 Minutes

1. Check unpaid money.
2. Check overdue tasks.
3. Check leads not contacted.
4. Ask AI for a midday rescue list.
5. Send follow-up messages.

## Evening: 15 Minutes

1. Paste what happened today.
2. Ask AI for the end-of-day closeout.
3. Assign tomorrow morning's first tasks.
4. Update money and leak records.

## Rule

If it is in your head, it is at risk.

Voice note it, screenshot it, or put it into Airtable.
