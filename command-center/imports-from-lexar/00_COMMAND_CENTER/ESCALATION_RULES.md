# Escalation Rules

The team should make routine decisions without waiting on the owner. Escalate only when risk crosses the limits below.

## Decide Without Owner Approval

The Acting Manager may decide:

- routine scheduling
- normal customer follow-up
- task assignments
- SOP-compliant refunds or credits within the approved limit
- standard discounts already listed in the sales playbook
- employee coaching and retraining
- vendor follow-up
- non-urgent process improvements

Every decision must be logged in the Decision Log.

## Escalate Same Day

Escalate in the daily summary when:

- a deal is stuck for more than 48 hours
- a customer issue is unresolved by end of day
- an employee misses a deadline twice in one week
- a lead source stops producing
- a payment is overdue
- a task depends on owner-only access

## Escalate Immediately

Call or message the owner immediately for:

- legal threat
- fraud concern
- account lockout
- payment processor, bank, card, email, domain, CRM, or phone system outage
- public complaint or reputation risk
- customer cancellation that could cost significant revenue
- employee no-show that affects customer delivery
- spending above the approved limit
- discount, refund, or promise outside approved rules
- anything involving passwords, API keys, or sensitive access

## Required Escalation Format

Use this format:

```text
Issue:
Business impact:
Money at risk:
Customer impact:
What we already tried:
Options:
Recommended decision:
Deadline:
```
