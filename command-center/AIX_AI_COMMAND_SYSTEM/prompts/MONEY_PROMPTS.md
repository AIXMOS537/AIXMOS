# Money Prompts

## Daily Money Check

```text
Act as my personal and business money operator.

Review this money data:
[paste bills, cards, income, expenses, or dashboard data]

Tell me:
1. What is due in the next 7 days.
2. What is overdue.
3. What needs to be paid first.
4. What can wait.
5. What should be cancelled, reduced, disputed, or renegotiated.
6. Which business has money stuck.
7. Which personal expense is leaking money.
8. What exact actions I should take today.
```

## Credit Card Paydown

```text
Act as my credit card strategist.

Review these cards:
[paste card name, balance, limit, due date, APR, minimum payment]

Rank them by urgency. Consider utilization, due dates, APR, business importance, and cash available.

Tell me:
- which card to pay first
- how much to pay if I have limited cash
- which cards to stop using
- which cards can stay on autopay
- what payment dates to move
```

## Expense Cut List

```text
Act as my expense auditor.

Review these expenses:
[paste expenses]

Label each as:
- revenue producing
- required operating cost
- nice to have
- waste
- unknown

Give me a cut list, keep list, and investigate list.
```

## Missed Money Review

```text
Act as my leak detector.

Review this business activity:
[paste leads, bookings, payments, tasks, messages, cancelled deals, or dashboard data]

Find money leaks:
- missed follow-ups
- unpaid invoices
- late fees
- poor close rate
- customer delays
- team delays
- cancelled bookings
- idle vehicles
- bad ad spend
- missing documents
- refund risk

For each leak, tell me:
- estimated money lost or at risk
- cause
- owner
- fix today
- automation or SOP to prevent it
```
