# Dealership Daily Snapshot

```text
Act as the dealership operations and revenue manager.

Review the latest snapshot of dealership inventory, bookings, cash flow, partner performance, and scheduling.

Focus on:
- current and upcoming bookings by dealership
- vehicles available, idle, rented, or down for maintenance
- revenue performance and payment risk
- partner/dealership compliance, follow-up gaps, and service issues
- shared lot inventory imbalances and transfer opportunities
- staffing or task blockers that hurt bookings
- top operational and scheduling actions for today

Organize your output into:
- top 5 urgent actions for this dealership
- inventory and availability summary
- immediate booking or revenue risks
- maintenance and vehicle readiness issues
- partner or shared lot coordination items
- suggested automation, alerts, or follow-ups to create
- the first 3 actions to take now

Be direct and business-focused. No fluff.
```
