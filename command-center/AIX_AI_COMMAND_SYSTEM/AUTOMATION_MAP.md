# Automation Map

Start with automations that protect money.

## Priority 1: Money

Create these first:

- Bill due reminder: 7 days before, 3 days before, due day.
- Credit card due reminder: 7 days before, 3 days before, due day.
- High card utilization alert: when balance is above 30%, 50%, and 80%.
- Expense review alert: when an expense is tagged Unknown or Waste.
- Overdue payment alert: daily until resolved.
- Subscription review: monthly.

## Priority 2: Car Rentals

- New lead creates Airtable record.
- New lead gets immediate text and email.
- No response in 2 hours creates follow-up task.
- Booking confirmed sends payment link.
- Payment due sends reminders.
- Return date sends customer reminder.
- Late return triggers team alert.
- Vehicle maintenance due creates task.
- Idle vehicle for 24 hours triggers marketing action.

## Priority 3: Credit Repair And Funding

- New lead creates client record.
- Missing documents trigger reminders.
- No response in 24 hours creates follow-up.
- Client status change sends next-step message.
- Funding lead gets eligibility checklist.
- Approved or denied result creates next action.

## Priority 4: Ecommerce

- New order creates tracking record.
- Delayed fulfillment creates alert.
- Refund request creates review task.
- Abandoned cart creates follow-up.
- Repeat buyer gets upsell message.

## Suggested Zapier Or Make Flow

Trigger:
- form submitted
- email received
- payment failed
- due date approaching
- Airtable status changed

Actions:
- create Airtable record
- update status
- send SMS or email
- create task
- notify team
- ask AI to draft message
- add calendar reminder

## AI Agent Jobs

Daily Operator:
- reads dashboard exports
- finds priorities
- drafts actions

Money Operator:
- reviews bills, cards, expenses, and income
- identifies risk

Follow-Up Operator:
- finds stale leads and unpaid balances
- drafts messages

Team Operator:
- turns chaos into assigned tasks
- checks overdue work
