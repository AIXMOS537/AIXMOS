# Discovery Call Script

Use this to qualify, diagnose, and close without rambling.

## Opening

```text
Thanks for taking the time today. I want to understand what you are trying to solve, what timeline matters, and whether we are a good fit. If it makes sense, I will walk you through the next step clearly.
```

## Qualification Questions

- What made you reach out now?
- What are you trying to accomplish?
- What have you already tried?
- What happens if this is not solved in the next 30 to 90 days?
- Who else is involved in the decision?
- What budget range are you expecting?
- What timeline are you working with?
- What would make this a clear win for you?

## Diagnose

Listen for:

- urgency
- money motivation
- pain
- authority
- trust concerns
- timeline
- objections

## Offer Bridge

```text
Based on what you shared, the main issue is [problem]. The outcome you want is [outcome]. The next best step is [offer/plan], because it gets you from [current state] to [desired state] without [main risk].
```

## Ask For Decision

```text
Does this sound like the right direction?
```

If yes:

```text
Good. The next step is [payment/agreement/documents/scheduling]. I can send that now and stay with you while you complete it.
```

If they hesitate:

```text
What part are you unsure about: the fit, the price, the timing, or trusting that this will work?
```

## Closeout

Before ending the call, confirm:

- decision
- next action
- owner
- deadline
- follow-up time
