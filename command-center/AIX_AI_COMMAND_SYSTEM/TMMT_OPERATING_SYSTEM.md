# TMMT Operating System

TMMT runs through five connected systems.

## 1. Command Center

Purpose: decide what matters today and make sure nothing important is stuck.

Files:

- `00_COMMAND_CENTER/DAILY_MANAGER_DASHBOARD.md`
- `00_COMMAND_CENTER/ESCALATION_RULES.md`
- `00_COMMAND_CENTER/WEEKLY_OWNER_UPDATE.md`

Tables:

- Team Tasks
- Decision Log
- Money Leaks

## 2. Sales Engine

Purpose: turn leads into closed deals through a repeatable process.

Files:

- `02_SALES_ENGINE/sales_pipeline.md`
- `02_SALES_ENGINE/discovery_call_script.md`
- `02_SALES_ENGINE/follow_up_sequences.md`
- `02_SALES_ENGINE/objection_bank.md`

Tables:

- Leads Deals
- Income
- Money Leaks

## 3. Training Academy

Purpose: train employees by role while they do real work.

Files:

- `03_TRAINING_ACADEMY/role_index.md`
- `03_TRAINING_ACADEMY/30_day_onboarding.md`

Tables:

- Employees
- Training Progress

## 4. Operations Manual

Purpose: make repeated work predictable.

Files:

- `04_OPERATIONS_MANUAL/sop_index.md`
- `04_OPERATIONS_MANUAL/daily_manager_routine.md`

Tables:

- Team Tasks
- Decision Log

## 5. Growth Lab

Purpose: test growth moves without distracting the team from revenue.

Rules:

- No growth experiment starts without an owner.
- No experiment runs without a success metric.
- No experiment continues after 14 days without a result review.
- Anything that creates repeatable revenue becomes an SOP.

## Weekly Rhythm

Monday:

- Review pipeline, unpaid money, and top operational risks.
- Assign weekly targets by person.

Tuesday through Thursday:

- Run daily manager routine.
- Push sales follow-up and fulfillment quality.

Friday:

- Send weekly owner update.
- Review training progress.
- Log decisions and SOP gaps.

Weekend:

- Only urgent customer, money, or access issues should interrupt the owner.
