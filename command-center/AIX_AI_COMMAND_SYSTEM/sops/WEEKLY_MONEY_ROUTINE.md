# Weekly Money Routine

Run this once per week.

## Step 1: Update Balances

Update:

- bank accounts
- credit cards
- payment processors
- cash on hand
- unpaid invoices
- bills due
- overdue bills

## Step 2: Review Expenses

Tag each expense:

- revenue producing
- required operating cost
- nice to have
- waste
- unknown

## Step 3: Review Credit Cards

For every card, update:

- balance
- limit
- utilization
- due date
- minimum payment
- APR
- autopay

## Step 4: Find Leaks

Look for:

- late fees
- card interest
- subscriptions
- unpaid customer balances
- missed follow-ups
- cancelled deals
- idle vehicles
- ad spend with no result

## Step 5: Ask AI

Use:

```text
Act as my CFO and leak detector. Review this weekly money data. Tell me what to pay, cut, collect, delay, renegotiate, and automate next week.
```
