# TMMT Operator Credit Education Program
**Owner Use Only — Internal Training Curriculum**

---

## Program Overview

This curriculum is designed for TMMT workers selected by the owner to enter the Operator Program. Completion prepares them for vehicle financing, personal credit ownership, and eventual business credit access.

**Program Goal:** Move each operator from credit repair → vehicle financed in their name → business credit established → full operator status.

---

## Curriculum — 8 Modules

| Module | Title | Core Topic |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | How Credit Scores Work | Factors, weights, score ranges, the 3 bureaus |
| 02 | What's Hurting Your Score Right Now | Collections, late payments, charge-offs, disputes |
| 03 | Payment History — Why It's 35% | The most important factor, autopay, 7-year rule |
| 04 | Credit Utilization — The Number Most People Miss | The 30% rule, timing, limit increases |
| 05 | How the Auto Financing Process Works | Steps from prep to title transfer, loan terms |
| 06 | What Lenders Look At Beyond the Score | 5 C's of credit, income, DTI, bank statements |
| 07 | What Owning a Financed Vehicle Does Long-Term | Score progression, refinancing, doors it opens |
| 08 | Building Business Credit After Personal Is Solid | Entity setup, vendor tradelines, business loans |

---

## Delivery Format

- Each module is a standalone document
- Assign one module per week or per check-in
- Each module ends with an Action Step the operator completes before the next session
- Owner reviews action step completion at each check-in

---

## Operator Pipeline Stages

```
Enrolled → Credit Active → Score Milestone (650) → Financing Ready → Vehicle Signed Over → Operator
```
