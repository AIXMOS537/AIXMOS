# TMMT Business Brain

This document is your command center. It connects your custom agents, business workflows, and daily operations into one place.

## What this looks like

- A single home page with the main business areas: Car Rentals, Credit Repair, Funding Guidance, Lease-to-Own, Document Editing, and Video Editing.
- A set of agent definitions in the workspace that can be invoked for specific tasks.
- A living plan that grows as you add processes, templates, forms, and marketing assets.
- A place to store priorities, daily actions, and the systems your business uses.

## Your agents

These agent definitions live in the workspace root:

- `d:\document-media-edit.agent.md` — for documents, contracts, signing, and image editing.
- `d:\video-edit.agent.md` — for video editing planning and production guidance.
- `d:\tmm-business-command-center.agent.md` — for TMMT operations, car rentals, credit repair, funding, and lease-to-own planning.

## How to be the agent inside the business

1. Use consistent language: think of the AI as your business partner.
2. Keep this dashboard up to date with goals, status, and core processes.
3. Ask the right questions: what do we need to do today, what asset needs editing, which customer flow needs improvement.
4. Use the agent files as the brain’s operating rules. When you need help, call their capabilities:
   - "Use TMMT Business Command Center to build our rental operations workflow."
   - "Use Document Media Editor to edit our lease-to-own contract."
   - "Use Video Editor to plan a promo video for our credit repair service."
5. Store templates, checklists, and operating procedures here so the AI can reference them.
