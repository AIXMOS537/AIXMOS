Requirements

What makes a standout AIXMOS operator?

We optimize for dependable humans who amplify the playbook — judgment, pacing, and follow-through beat heroics.

  • Presence: You can hold live blocks, onsite or remote, without ghosting timelines.
  • Reliability: SLA windows and escalation paths matter — we track commitments, not intentions.
  • Communication: Structured updates beat chaos; terse, honest status is a feature.
  • Operational literacy: Comfortable with ticketing, CRMs, and annotated handoffs clients can replay.
  • Integrity under ambiguity: When the model is uncertain, you surface risk instead of smoothing it away.
